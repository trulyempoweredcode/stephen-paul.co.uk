Life and personal coaching

Coaching

Specialist sessions to review aspects of one’s life journey so far and identify future directions. The individual is helped to release his/her inner potential and overcome personal obstacles to achievement of personal hopes and goals.

A positive approach

Making significant changes in a short time

This is a very positive approach to reviewing your life and making effective changes. It is possible to review your present situation, get a real sense of a life vision that is right for you, and make significant life changes in quite a short period of time.

I do an increasing amount of personal and life coaching which can be a very rewarding opportunity to reflect on your life so far and your hopes and aspirations.
